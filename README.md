# MINIMALSOFT TOOLWORKS TECHNOLOGIES

## MinimalSoft FLOW

**Evidence-first AI workspace for RFP, tender, and proposal response workflows.**

MinimalSoft FLOW is a B2B SaaS product being developed by **MINIMALSOFT TOOLWORKS TECHNOLOGIES**, a technology company focused on practical AI-powered business workflows.

🌐 **Company:** https://minimalsofttoolworks.com

---

## What MinimalSoft FLOW Does

MinimalSoft FLOW is designed to help organizations work with complex business documents and turn requirements into structured, evidence-backed proposal work.

Core workflow areas include:

- RFP and tender document analysis
- Requirement and obligation extraction
- Evidence discovery and organization
- Requirement-to-evidence mapping
- Proposal response preparation
- Structured response workflows
- Workspace-based project organization
- AI-assisted business document workflows

The product is being developed as a serious B2B SaaS platform, with an emphasis on traceability, structured workflows, and evidence rather than unsupported AI-generated claims.

---

## Product Philosophy

### Evidence first

Business-critical AI output should be grounded in source material whenever possible.

### Structured workflows

Complex RFP and proposal work is treated as a workflow rather than a single chat interaction.

### Human control

AI is intended to accelerate analysis and preparation while keeping users in control of final business decisions and submissions.

### Secure by design

Production credentials, private application code, customer information, API keys, OAuth secrets, and other sensitive material are not stored in this public repository.
