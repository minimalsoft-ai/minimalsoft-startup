# Development

## Product

**MinimalSoft FLOW** is an evidence-first AI workspace for RFP, tender, and proposal response workflows.

## Development Direction

Development is focused on making complex business-document workflows more structured, traceable, and efficient.

Key areas include:

1. Document understanding
2. Requirement extraction
3. Evidence organization
4. Requirement/evidence mapping
5. AI-assisted response preparation
6. Human review
7. Secure SaaS infrastructure
8. Scalable cloud-native delivery

## Engineering Approach

MinimalSoft favors:

- Clear separation between product concerns
- Small, maintainable services
- Secure handling of credentials
- Explicit data boundaries
- Evidence-backed AI workflows
- Observable and testable infrastructure
- Incremental product development

## Public vs. Private Code

This repository is intentionally separate from the private production repository.

The public repository exists to document the company and product at a safe level. Production source code remains private.

## Status

MinimalSoft FLOW is actively being developed. Product capabilities and infrastructure may evolve as the platform moves toward broader production readiness.
